What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect:

  • your email address,
  • your first name,
  • your country.

We do not collect any other personal data through this website. The site itself does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Why we collect it

We use your data to run the waitlist: to tell you about Drop 001 and future Teyo boards, and to give you early access before a release. Your country helps us plan shipping.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you join the waitlist (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data. After that we remove it from our email list without undue delay.

Who processes it for us

Our email list is hosted by Kit (ConvertKit LLC, United States), which acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Kit stores the data and sends our emails. It also records basic email activity, such as opens and clicks.

Because Kit is based in the United States, your data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy,
  • correct it,
  • have it deleted,
  • restrict its processing,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. For anything else, write to contact@teyo.surf.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (PUODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

Is it required

No. Giving your data is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot add you to the waitlist.

Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions or create profiles based on your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
