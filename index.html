The board

01 / 07
  1. 01

    Embedded grip

    Custom grip, set slightly into the deck. Locks your feet in, adds almost nothing to the weight, never peels like a pad.

  2. 02

    Double concave deck

    Two shallow concaves under your feet. More control over rail-to-rail pumping, better feel through the board.

  3. 03

    Glass-reinforced foil boxes

    Custom, light, reinforced with fibreglass. Glass stretches further before it breaks, so it absorbs impacts and repeated loads better than carbon. SCS system compatible for easy foil mounting.

  4. 04

    Bottom channels

    Longitudinal channels laminated into the bottom work as ribs, reinforcing the bottom skin and adding torsional stiffness while keeping the weight down.

  5. 05

    Original artwork

    Every graphic is drawn by hand by the shaper, a painter by training, so no two boards look the same. A board with a soul, built first and foremost for top performance on the water.

  6. 06

    Durable carbon–PVC sandwich

    A dense, closed-cell 80 kg/m³ PVC foam core. With carbon skins top and bottom, reinforced under the foil boxes, it forms a stiff, durable sandwich that does not soak up water.

  7. 07

    Light by design

    Every gram was questioned, from the core to the grip. Less mass means less inertia: the board reacts instantly to every pump, carries speed more efficiently and swings naturally under your feet.
